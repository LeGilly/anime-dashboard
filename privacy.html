Privacy

Anime Dashboard · last updated 21 November 2026

In short

Anime Dashboard has no accounts and no database. Your lists are kept only in your own browser. Signing in is optional and only lets you see and edit your own MyAnimeList list from here.

What's kept in your browser

  • The lists you load (yours, and the last one you browsed), for up to 30 days.
  • Details about titles (covers, genres, sequels), to avoid asking MyAnimeList again, for up to 60 days.
  • Your settings (English titles, blurring 18+ covers).
  • Your colour theme (for example "purple.dark"), in a small cookie shared by every legilly.com site so the theme is the same on all of them. It holds only the theme name.
  • A short record of the last few hundred requests to MyAnimeList (time, kind of request, how long it took and whether it worked; no names), to understand slowdowns. It only leaves your browser if you use Settings → Copy request log and share it yourself.

You can remove all of it at any time with Settings → Forget this list, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

If you sign in with MyAnimeList

  • You approve access on MyAnimeList's own page. Your MyAnimeList password never comes to this site.
  • MyAnimeList gives back an access key. It's kept in an encrypted cookie that only the dashboard's relay (api.legilly.com) can read: not this page, not other pages, not other sites.
  • The key is used only to read your list (including private entries) and to make the changes you ask for: episodes, scores, status, dates, adding and removing titles.
  • It stays for up to 60 days, or until you choose Settings → Sign out. You can also remove the dashboard's access from your MyAnimeList account settings.

The relay

The relay is a small program on Cloudflare that passes requests between your browser and MyAnimeList. It doesn't store your lists, your sign-in or anything else. To understand MyAnimeList's slowdowns, it keeps a short log for a few days, visible only to the site's owner: each request it received (including the MyAnimeList username of a list you load, which is public on MyAnimeList anyway), and for each request it made to MyAnimeList, the kind of request, MyAnimeList's answer and timings. Your sign-in key is never written in a readable form. Cloudflare may also keep short technical logs (such as which addresses made requests) as part of running its service.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared. Your data only goes between your browser, the relay and MyAnimeList. There are no ads, trackers or analytics.

Questions

Anime Dashboard is a fan-made project, not affiliated with MyAnimeList. The code is public on GitHub, where you can also ask questions.
